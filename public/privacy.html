Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-29  ·  Potion Siege  ·  support@gamestao.com  ·  中文

This policy explains what data the game Potion Siege collects, why, and what you can do about it. It applies wherever you play the game: on our own site (p.gamestao.com), on game sites such as CrazyGames, Playgama, itch.io or Newgrounds, and in the iPhone app. We follow the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the applicable data protection law.

There is no account and no sign-up, and the game never asks who you are. Your progress and settings stay on your device (on CrazyGames and Playgama, in the portal's own save). The game sends anonymous play statistics to our own server, linked to a random install ID rather than to you; in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland only after you allow it, and everywhere you can switch them off in Settings. Ads appear only on CrazyGames, on Playgama and in the iPhone app, and in the iPhone app they are never personalised.

1. Who is responsible

The controller is Tao Wang, an independent sole trader (not a company) based in Germany, reachable at support@gamestao.com.

3. What we do not collect

  • No account, no name, no email address, no password.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking. The statistics above go to our own server only. The game contains no Google Analytics, no Firebase and no tracking script. The iPhone app contains Google's ad software for the ads described in §5, limited to non-personalised ads.
  • No advertising ID and no profile of you, and no tracking across other sites or apps. The iPhone app never asks to track you, so neither we nor Google get Apple's advertising ID.
  • No precise location. We record only the country Cloudflare derives from your IP address (§2), nothing finer: no region, city or coordinates. To know whether to ask before sending play statistics, the game looks at the country CrazyGames reports or, elsewhere, at your device's time zone; this stays on your device. Google may estimate a rough location from your IP address to serve ads in the iPhone app (§5).
  • No payment details (§6).
  • No sale or sharing of your data for anyone else's purposes.

4. Stored on your device

The game keeps a few items in your browser's local storage. No cookies.

  • mb.save: your progress and settings. On CrazyGames and Playgama it lives in the portal's save, which follows a signed-in player to other devices.
  • mb.player: the random install ID (created only once play statistics may be sent), the day you first played, how many times you have played, which first steps you have reached, and where you first came from.
  • mb.privacy: your answer about play statistics.
  • ps.adFree (iPhone app only): whether you own the Ad-free card, as the App Store last told the app.

In the iPhone app these items are also copied into the app's own storage, so iOS clearing the web storage loses nothing, and the app downloads game updates from our server at ps-ota.gamestao.com. Clearing the site's data in your browser, or deleting the app, removes all of it; the game then starts over with a new install ID.

5. Advertising

On our own site, and on sites such as itch.io or Newgrounds that host our files, the game shows no ads.

On CrazyGames, ads are served by CrazyGames: optional rewarded videos and a short break ad between battles. CrazyGames' privacy policy covers those ads and any consent they need. We receive nothing about you from them, only whether an ad finished.

On Playgama, ads are served by Playgama in the same way: optional rewarded videos and a short break ad between battles, under Playgama's privacy policy and its consent. We learn only whether an ad finished, and whether an ad blocker stopped it.

In the iPhone app, ads are served by Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos and a short break ad between battles. Every ad request asks for non-personalised ads only, and the app never shows Apple's tracking prompt. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form (its User Messaging Platform) asks you first, before any ad is requested, and the Ad privacy choices button in the game's Settings opens it again so you can change your answer. To serve, count and protect these ads Google processes your IP address, a device identifier that only apps from the same developer share (not the advertising ID), which ads you saw and whether you tapped them, and technical and crash data from its ad software, as described in how Google uses information from apps that use its services. With the Ad-free card no ad is shown and ad rewards are paid at once.

6. Purchases

The only thing sold for real money is the iPhone app's Ad-free card, bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment, Ask to Buy and refunds. The app only learns from Apple whether you own the card; we never see your card, your Apple ID, your name or your address.

7. Who else processes the data

  • The game site you play on (for example CrazyGames, Playgama, itch.io or Newgrounds). It hosts the page; CrazyGames also saves your progress and serves ads: CrazyGames privacy policy.
  • Playgama, when you play there: it saves your progress, serves the ads (§5), and receives a short form of the game events of §2 for its own statistics: the tutorial's steps and each battle's start and end (stage or mode, wave, won or lost, time played, kills, merges, best potion level, wall left, the battle count), with the device type (phone, tablet or desktop, touch or not), the session and day counts and the game version. These go to Playgama through its own SDK, also before you answer about play statistics, and Playgama's privacy policy covers them: Playgama privacy policy.
  • Apple, in the iPhone app: the App Store and the purchase of the Ad-free card: Apple privacy policy.
  • Google, in the iPhone app: the ads and the consent form (§5): Google privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare: delivers the game and its updates and carries the traffic to our server.
  • Hetzner Online (Germany): runs our server, where the statistics are stored.

Cloudflare and Hetzner process the data for us under data processing agreements. Where data is processed outside the EEA, this happens under the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

8. How long we keep it

Play statistics, load counts and feedback are stored as one file per day on our server in Germany. They contain no name, address or other detail that says who you are. Each day's file is deleted automatically after 14 months, sooner if you ask us to delete yours (§9). A daily backup copy of each day's file is kept on the same server for 30 days. Battle replays are deleted automatically after 15 days, or sooner when the space set aside for them is full (the oldest first), and are not backed up. The server's own logs record no IP addresses and are overwritten once they reach about 30 MB. Connection data is not stored (§2).

9. Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction and a portable copy, and you can object to processing based on our legitimate interest at any time. Consent you gave by allowing play statistics or by sending feedback can be withdrawn at any time, without affecting what was sent before.

Because we do not know who you are, we can only find your data by your install ID. The game shows it in Settings → Player ID, with a Copy button where your device allows (it is also the id in mb.player, §4). To see or delete your data, email support@gamestao.com with that ID. If Settings says there is no player ID, nothing has been sent from that device. To stop being counted from now on, switch off Share stats in Settings; the ID stays shown there so you can still ask us to delete what was sent. Clearing the site's data or deleting the app also removes the ID from your device, after which nothing links you to it, so note it first if you want your data deleted. We answer within 30 days, free of charge. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority.

10. Children

The game asks no one for personal data: there is no account, no chat and nothing to type except the optional feedback line. Ads in the iPhone app are non-personalised. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top always shows the current version.

12. Contact

For any privacy question or request: support@gamestao.com.

游戏统计、加载计数和反馈按天存成文件，放在我们位于德国的服务器上，其中没有姓名、地址或其他能说明你是谁的信息。每天的文件保存 14 个月后自动删除；你要求删除你的数据时会更早删除（见第 9 节）。每天的文件在同一台服务器上另有一份备份，保留 30 天。战斗回放 15 天后自动删除，为它们预留的空间满了时会更早删除（最旧的先删），且不做备份。服务器自己的日志不记录 IP 地址，累计约 30 MB 后就会被覆盖。连接数据不保存（见第 2 节）。

9. 你的权利

你可以要求查阅、更正、删除、限制处理你的数据，或获取一份可携带的副本，也可以随时反对基于我们正当利益的处理。你通过允许游戏统计或发送反馈给出的同意可以随时撤回，撤回不影响之前已发送的数据。

由于我们不知道你是谁，只能通过安装 ID 找到你的数据。游戏在“设置”→“玩家 ID”里显示它，设备允许时还有“复制”按钮（它也就是 mb.player 里的 id，见第 4 节）。如需查看或删除你的数据，请把这个 ID 发到 support@gamestao.com。如果“设置”里显示没有玩家 ID，说明这台设备从未发送过数据。如果想从现在起不再被统计，关闭“设置”里的“分享统计”即可；ID 仍会显示在那里，方便你要求我们删除已发送的数据。清除本站数据或删除应用也会把 ID 从设备上删掉，之后就再也没有什么能把你和它联系起来，所以如果想让我们删除数据，请先记下它。我们会在 30 天内免费答复。你也有权向数据保护监管机构投诉。

10. 儿童

游戏不向任何人索要个人数据：没有账号，没有聊天，除了那一行可选的反馈，没有任何需要输入文字的地方。iPhone 应用里的广告都是非个性化的。如果你认为有儿童向我们发送了个人数据，请联系我们，我们会删除。
