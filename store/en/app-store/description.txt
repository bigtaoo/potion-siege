The clockwork army is marching on the alchemist's castle, and his potions have come to life to stop it.

Summon potion heroes onto the wall, then drag two matching potions together to merge them into a stronger one. Fire Tonics blast crowds, Frost Tonics slow them down, Storm Tonics chain lightning and Arcane Tonics never miss; later the Corrosion and Gale Tonics join the fight. Every upgrade offers three skill cards: build your run around one potion or mix them all.

Hold the wall against Tin Soldiers, Clock Mice, Boiler Golems, Ornithopters and Winder Spiders. Each enemy fears different potions, so check the waves coming up and plan your brew. When the bosses roll in, from the Steam Tank to the Gear King, tap the alchemist once he is fully charged and he throws a flask bomb.

Between battles, level up your potions, upgrade the workshop and push through five chapters, from the Factory Outskirts to the Clock Tower.

FEATURES
• Quick merge-defense battles that last a few minutes
• 6 potion heroes, 30+ skill cards, ultimate potions with bonus skills
• 5 clockwork enemy types, each with its own weaknesses
• 5 bosses, each with its own trick
• 50 stages in 5 chapters
• Endless mode: how many waves can your wall hold?
• Daily challenge, daily tasks and daily rewards
• Workshop upgrades, a codex and achievements
• Plays in 15 languages
